How I work

Discovery to delivery, without losing the thread.

Discovery, analysis, design, validation, and release. All structured to keep the original business need visible through every stage.

01
Discovery

Define the problem before designing the solution

Capture business context, surface conflicting assumptions, and identify the decisions that need to be made before design begins.

02
Analysis

Structure complexity into workable logic

Break down processes, data flows, rules, and exceptions. Produce specs, maps, and acceptance criteria that build teams can actually use.

03
Delivery

Keep business intent intact through build

Translate requirements into sprint-ready items. Support design decisions and keep cross-functional teams aligned through code complete.

04
Assurance

Verify readiness before the release window

Coordinate testing, defect resolution, and stakeholder sign-off. Make sure the release is clean before the handoff window opens.
