Executive Summary

A concise view of the migration challenge, my ownership, and the value delivered.

Business Challenge

Undocumented migration rules, inconsistent source data behaviour, and unclear ownership of data decisions created significant risk ahead of go-live.

My Role

Led migration analysis, defined field mappings, reconciled business rules, tracked gaps, coordinated validation runs, and supported cutover readiness and sign-off.

Solution

A structured source-to-target migration flow with governed rules, documented gaps, controlled load runs, reconciliation validation, and sign-off evidence at each milestone.

Business Value

Improved migration traceability, reduced data risk, clear rule ownership, and a structured readiness process that gave stakeholders confidence at cutover.

My Contributions

  • Led source system assessment to document data volumes, quality issues, structural inconsistencies, and migration risks before mapping began.
  • Defined field-level mapping specifications connecting source attributes to target model fields with transformation logic and exception handling.
  • Facilitated business rule sessions to resolve migration decisions, defaulting logic, and data exception treatment with business and data teams.
  • Tracked and managed a gap register covering unmapped records, missing values, and outstanding decisions requiring business input.
  • Coordinated validation runs, reconciliation reviews, and business sign-off at each migration milestone through to cutover.

Key Deliverables

  • Source data assessment and profiling report
  • Field-level mapping specification document
  • Business rules and transformation logic documentation
  • Gap register and outstanding decision log
  • Validation run results and reconciliation evidence
  • Cutover checklist and readiness sign-off record
