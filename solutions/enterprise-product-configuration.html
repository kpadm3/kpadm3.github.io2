Executive Summary

A concise view of the business problem, my ownership, and the value delivered.

Business Challenge

Interdependent product rules were distributed across documents, spreadsheets, and product knowledge, making configuration inconsistent and difficult to scale.

My Role

Led analysis, structured the backlog, defined rule and validation logic, aligned stakeholders, and supported delivery through UAT and release readiness.

Solution

Created a governed configuration flow connecting product data, business rules, validation controls, approvals, and ERP-ready outputs.

Business Value

Improved consistency, traceability, cross-team alignment, and release confidence while reducing manual interpretation and rework.
