AI / Agent / Human
AI

Reads product rules, identifies configuration conflicts and explains what caused each exception. Past approved exceptions are referenced in future checks.

Agent

Orchestrates validation, routes exceptions to the right owner and logs approved decisions for future review.

Human

Product owners approve ambiguous configurations. Overrides are allowed and every decision with its reason is captured.

Technical design
MVP scenarios
Scenario 1 Valid configuration. All rules satisfied, no conflicts detected. Pass
Scenario 2 Hard rule violation. Product A with Option X is a prohibited combination. Fail
Scenario 3 Ambiguous configuration. AI detects a likely conflict with Rule 27 at medium confidence and routes to the SME with supporting evidence. Review
Controls and governance
AI does not override hard validation rules. Those remain authoritative.
Insufficient context triggers a review request, not a guess.
Human override is available at any step.
Every decision and override reason is logged for audit.
Implementation note

Azure OpenAI can provide the managed LLM layer for this design. The workflow, deterministic controls, human approvals and orchestration are designed to remain portable to equivalent enterprise LLM platforms.
