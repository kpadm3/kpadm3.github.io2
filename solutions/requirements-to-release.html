Executive Summary

A concise view of the delivery challenge, my ownership, and the value delivered.

Business Challenge

Complex changes needed stronger traceability between business objectives, delivery artefacts, test evidence, and release decisions across multiple teams and sprints.

My Role

Owned requirements, structured the backlog, defined acceptance criteria, led UAT planning, and managed release readiness across product, engineering, and QA teams.

Solution

Connected discovery through release with traceable backlog items, structured acceptance criteria, linked test evidence, and a governed release readiness process.

Business Value

Clearer delivery expectations, stronger acceptance evidence, reduced rework, and better accountability across product, engineering, and QA from discovery through go-live.
