Executive Summary

A concise view of the reporting challenge, my ownership, and the value delivered.

Business Challenge

Recurring reports required repeated manual extraction, spreadsheet manipulation, and validation, consuming significant analyst and business time each reporting cycle.

My Role

Defined reporting requirements, designed the extraction and transformation flow, specified validation rules, and structured the reusable user-facing workflow and output format.

Solution

A governed source-to-output flow with controlled extraction, structured transformation, built-in validation, and a consistent user-facing report refresh process.

Business Value

Reduced repetitive manual handling, improved report consistency, and gave users a reliable, reusable process with less dependence on individual analyst effort each cycle.

My Contributions

  • Facilitated workshops with business users to document current reporting pain points, volume, frequency, and output requirements.
  • Analysed source files to define extraction logic, field structure, and data quality considerations before transformation design.
  • Specified transformation rules, calculation logic, aggregation patterns, and output field definitions for the automated flow.
  • Designed built-in validation checks and exception handling to reduce manual verification effort per reporting cycle.
  • Structured the user-facing workflow to support consistent, repeatable report refresh by non-technical business users.

Key Deliverables

  • Transformation and calculation rule definitions
  • Validation logic and exception-handling rules
  • User guide and step-by-step refresh workflow
