Technical design
MVP scenarios
Scenario 1Clean report. Validated input completes the reporting workflow with no exceptions.Ready
Scenario 2Invalid input. Missing or malformed source data fails deterministic validation.Fail
Scenario 3Significant variance. A validated metric changes beyond an approved threshold. AI summarizes the movement for analyst review.Review
Scenario 4Management question. Copilot answers a controlled question such as "What changed this month?" using approved reporting data only.Insight
Scenario 5Incorrect AI interpretation. Analyst rejects or edits AI commentary and records the reason.Override
Scenario 6Prior-period restatement. Previously reported data is resubmitted. The workflow detects the changed version, identifies impacted metrics and routes a revised report for approval.Restatement
Controls and governance
AI runs only after data passes validation.
Core calculations and thresholds remain deterministic.
Business rules are defined independently of Excel and Power Query so the logic can move to a more stable platform later.
Copilot uses only approved reporting data.
AI commentary is never published without human review.
Restatements require explicit approval.
Overrides and reasons are logged.
