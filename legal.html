Legal, Privacy & Feedback

This page explains how this portfolio currently handles privacy, use of site content, external links and feedback. It is written for a personal professional portfolio rather than as corporate legal boilerplate.

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Privacy

This portfolio is currently a static website. It does not currently provide user accounts, a server-side contact form or a site-operated visitor database.

  • A theme preference may be stored locally in your browser so the site can remember light or dark mode.
  • Choosing an external link such as LinkedIn, GitHub or Credly takes you to a third-party service that operates under its own privacy practices.
  • If analytics, an interactive AI assistant, persistent session features or additional contact tools are enabled later, this notice will be updated before those features are treated as part of the public launch state.
